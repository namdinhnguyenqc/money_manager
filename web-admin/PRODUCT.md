# Product

## Register

product

## Users

TrọCare primarily serves Vietnamese boarding-house owners and their staff, with secondary surfaces for platform administrators, tenants, and prospective renters. Owners use the product repeatedly during operational work: managing properties and rooms, creating contracts and invoices, collecting payments, tracking cash flow, and responding to leads. The interface must let them understand status and complete the next action quickly, including on smaller screens and less reliable networks.

## Product Purpose

TrọCare digitizes room-rental operations in one dependable workspace. It connects property operations, contracts, billing, payments, financial records, and the public marketplace while preserving strict owner-level data isolation. Success means users can complete high-frequency workflows correctly with minimal training, recover safely from errors, and trust every financial or occupancy state shown by the system.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, clear, and approachable for Vietnamese users. The product communicates with calm confidence, uses familiar domain language, and prioritizes operational clarity over novelty.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI-generated SaaS dashboards, decorative glassmorphism, neon or gradient-heavy finance interfaces, excessive card nesting, gratuitous animation, and unfamiliar custom controls. The product must not feel playful when users are handling contracts, occupancy, invoices, or money.

## Design Principles

- Make the next operational action obvious without hiding the context needed to make a safe decision.
- Treat financial, contract, and occupancy state as auditable facts; never trade correctness for visual simplicity.
- Use one consistent interaction vocabulary across owner, admin, public, and mobile surfaces.
- Preserve user input and provide a clear recovery path whenever validation, connectivity, or authorization fails.
- Earn trust through plain Vietnamese language, predictable behavior, and restrained visual hierarchy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All primary workflows must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion preferences, semantic labels, and status communication that does not rely on color alone. Content and controls must remain usable on mobile viewports, under text zoom, and during slow or interrupted network conditions.
